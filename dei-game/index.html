Stage 1 · Explainer

D, E and I are three different things

D

Diversity

Who is in the room. The mix of backgrounds, identities, abilities, ages, beliefs and ways of thinking in a group.

Ask: whose perspective is missing?

E

Equity

Fair access to opportunity. People start from different places, so fairness sometimes means different support, not identical treatment.

Ask: does everyone have what they need to take part?

I

Inclusion

Whether people can take part fully once they are in the room: be heard, be credited, and bring their whole self without paying a social penalty.

Ask: who spoke, and who got the credit?

Explainer · Why bias is hard to see

Bias is mostly a shortcut, not a choice

Affinity bias

We warm to people who remind us of ourselves. "Culture fit" often hides it.

Name bias

In a 2004 US field study, résumés with white-sounding names got about 50% more callbacks than identical ones with Black-sounding names.

Microaggressions

Small, often well-meant remarks that signal "you don't belong here". Each is minor; the pile-up is not.
