# Common Ground

A single-file, interactive DEI (diversity, equity & inclusion) workbook. Open `index.html` in a browser; no build step.

Stages:
1. **Learn** – explainers for D, E and I, an equality vs equity vs "remove the barrier" demo, and common biases.
2. **Spot** – tap the biased phrases in a job ad (gendered, age, origin, ability and culture cues).
3. **Decide** – seven workplace scenarios; each choice scores Awareness, Empathy and Action with feedback.
4. **Reflect** – a 0–100 reflection score, radar chart, tips for the weakest areas, and a team board saved in the browser (workshop mode).

The score reflects choices made in the game. It is a conversation starter, not an assessment of people.
